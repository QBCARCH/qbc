A compact Belgrade apartment designed around one open living space. The living room, kitchen and dining bar are tied together by continuous oak surfaces and a dark graphite palette, with a slim metal shelving unit that separates the zones without closing them off.

Storage is built into wall-to-wall joinery, the kitchen sits in a timber niche with a breakfast bar, and the bedroom keeps the same calm tones with an upholstered headboard wall. The apartment feels larger than its footprint because every piece of storage has its place in the joinery.
