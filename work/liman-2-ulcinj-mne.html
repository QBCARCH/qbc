Located above Liman 2 beach in Ulcinj, on the Montenegrin coast, this residential and commercial building follows the steep, wooded slope instead of fighting it. The volume is broken into cascading terraces, so the apartments step down the hill and each one gets its own open terrace with planting and a view of the sea.

The material palette comes from the Adriatic tradition: local stone walls, white rendered volumes, timber shutters and planted parapets. Seen from the beach, the building reads as part of the hillside rather than as a single block.
