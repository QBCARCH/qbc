About QBC Architects /

Engineering functional aesthetics

Based in Belgrade, working internationally

Start a project→

Founded in Belgrade in 2010 by architects Vladica and Slavoljub Ljubić, QBC Architects designs houses, apartments and hospitality spaces, and resolves them in detail: a coordinated Revit model, drawings that contractors can price, and renders that show what will be built.

Our practice rests on engineering discipline and respect for each site. We do not stop at concepts: we develop technical documentation that is ready for execution. Our BIM workflow in Autodesk Revit lets us coordinate architecture with structure and building services, and find clashes on screen, before construction.

Architecture is a significant investment, so we design with costs in view, choosing materials and layouts that keep construction efficient without compromising the design. We plan the permitting path from the first sketch, so regulation shapes the design early instead of stopping it late.

Permits and regulation /

We know the Serbian permitting system from the inside.

Slavoljub Ljubić worked at the Serbian Ministry of Construction, so he knows the regulations and CEOP, the national e-permit system, from the inside.

For clients this means fewer surprises. Planning conditions, building permits and use permits are part of the schedule from day one, not something discovered along the way. For investors from abroad, we handle the Serbian side of the process.

Our approach

01

Designed to be built

Every concept is tested against structure, materials and construction method from the first sketch, so the design survives contact with the site.

02

BIM from day one

One Revit model drives plans, sections, schedules and renders. Clashes with structure and building services are found on screen, not on site.

03

Cost awareness

We specify materials and layouts with the budget in view, and say early when a decision will cost more.

04

Context first

Climate, topography and local building culture shape each design. No two projects start from the same template.
