Nestled in the serene mountain landscape of Divčibare, this contemporary residential estate redefines alpine living. The project consists of a spacious family home and a thoughtfully designed guest house, both crafted to harmonize flawlessly with their natural surroundings. Combining the warmth of Siberian larch with the sleekness of anthracite steel, the architecture offers a modern interpretation of the traditional mountain retreat.

The main residence is designed around natural light and open space, featuring impressive three-meter-high ceilings and a grand living area anchored by a central fireplace. It serves as a true private sanctuary, complete with a dedicated lower-level SPA and wellness zone that flows seamlessly onto an outdoor terrace. The accompanying guest house mirrors this sophisticated comfort, offering an open-plan layout, its own sauna, and a charming sleeping gallery.

Beyond its striking aesthetics, the estate is built for ultimate comfort and sustainability. With advanced thermal insulation, underfloor heating powered by heat pumps, and integrated smart home technology, it provides a highly energy-efficient haven. This project is not just a house; it is a meticulously engineered retreat where modern luxury meets the untamed beauty of nature.
