Architectural, Interior Design & Visualisation Services

We provide architecture, interior design and visualisation, from the first concept to supervision on site. One studio carries the project through design, permits and construction, so nothing gets lost between phases. Outside Serbia we work together with your local architect of record.

What we do /

Seven services, one studio

01Conceptual & Schematic DesignSite Analysis / Pattern Study / Massing and Form

We turn your brief into a clear spatial strategy, starting with a pattern study of the site and of the way the building will be used. This phase covers site analysis, the planning conditions and the first concepts: layout, massing and the relationships between spaces.

Site Analysis

Topography, orientation, views, access and the planning rules that apply to the plot.

Pattern Study

The recurring relationships of light, movement, privacy and gathering that the design has to answer.

Massing and Form

Early 3D models that test the volumes on the site, and the form that follows from the patterns.

02Design Development & 3D Modeling3D Model as Reference / Coordination with Engineers / Material Specification

We develop the design in a precise 3D model in Rhino. Materials are chosen, and structure and building services are coordinated with the engineers, so junctions are resolved in the model before they reach the site.

3D Model as Reference

Every dimension is checked in 3D, and plans, sections and elevations are taken from the model.

Coordination with Engineers

Structure and building services are checked against the architecture at each step.

Material Specification

Finishes and systems chosen for how they look, what they cost and how they will be built.

03Architectural VisualisationConcept Visuals / Material and Interior Studies / Presentation Imagery

Photorealistic images, from the first massing studies to the final choice of materials. Renders are made from the same 3D model as the drawings, so what you approve is what gets documented.

Concept Visuals

Early images that test massing, light and atmosphere before the design is fixed.

Material and Interior Studies

Close views of finishes, furniture and lighting, so decisions can be made with confidence.

Presentation Imagery

High-resolution images for investors, permit submissions and sales.

04Construction Documents & EngineeringArchitectural Drawings / Construction Details / Permit Documentation

Drawings, specifications and schedules that contractors can price and build from. Careful detailing means more accurate bids, fewer changes on site and documents that meet the applicable building codes.

Architectural Drawings

Plans, sections and elevations with the dimensions and notes a contractor needs to price the work.

Construction Details

Drawings of junctions, the thermal envelope and waterproofing, where most problems on site begin.

Permit Documentation

Documents that meet the applicable codes. In Serbia: IDR, PGD and PZI, submitted through the CEOP e-permit system.

05Construction Administration & OversightSite Visits / Submittal Review / Design Queries and Changes

After the building permit is issued, clients can engage us to supervise the construction of our own projects. We visit the site, review contractor submittals and answer technical questions, so the building is built as it was designed.

Site Visits

Regular visits to check that the work follows the approved design and documentation.

Submittal Review

Shop drawings and material samples checked against the specification.

Design Queries and Changes

Answers to contractors' technical questions, and proposed changes assessed against the approved design.

06Interior Architecture & Custom DetailingCustom Joinery / Lighting Design / Materials and Hardware

We carry the architectural idea into the interior: custom joinery, lighting and the choice of materials, so that the building and its interior read as one.

Custom Joinery

Cabinetry and built-in elements drawn to the geometry of each space.

Lighting Design

Lighting plans that follow how each space is used, by day and at night.

Materials and Hardware

Interior finishes, fixtures and hardware that follow the architectural idea.

07Feasibility Studies & Master PlanningSite Capacity / Variants and Density / Phasing

For larger sites we test what can be built before design begins: how much floor area the planning rules allow, how it can be placed on the land and in which order it can be built. Our pattern-based tools let us compare many variants at this stage, with their areas and indices side by side.

Site Capacity

The maximum buildable area under the planning rules, and the constraints that limit it.

Variants and Density

Several layouts with their areas and indices, so the investor can compare them by the numbers.

Phasing

A large development divided into stages that can be financed and built one after another.

Questions /

Frequently asked questions

01Do you work with clients outside Serbia?

Yes. Concept design, interior design and architectural visualisation can be done fully remotely, with video meetings in English. For a building outside Serbia we work together with your local architect of record, who handles the permit in that country. For projects in Serbia we handle the whole process.

02What do you mean by patterns?

Patterns are relationships that repeat in good buildings and places: how light enters, how people arrive, where they gather and where they withdraw, how a building meets the ground. We study them for each site before we draw a form. The method comes from our own research, and we are developing it into our own design software.

03What do you need to start a project?

The location of the plot or the property, any documents you already have for it, a short description of what you need, a rough budget and your target timeline. If you do not have all of it yet, send what you have and we will help you complete the brief.

04How are fees agreed?

You receive a written proposal before work begins. It lists the scope of services, the phases and the fee for each phase, so you know what you get and what it costs at every stage.

05How long does a project take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the project and on the permit procedure. A concept usually takes several weeks, and a full set of documents takes a few months. We give a schedule in the proposal and update it as the project moves forward.

06What is CEOP and how does it affect my project?

CEOP is the Serbian national e-permit system, through which building permits are applied for electronically. We know the regulations and the procedure well, so we plan the permit path from the first sketch and prepare documentation that meets the requirements.

07Do you supervise construction?

Yes, on our own projects. After the building permit is issued, clients can engage us to supervise the construction: site visits, review of contractor submittals and answers to technical questions. We are architects, not a contractor, so we do not build.

08Do you design interiors on their own?

Yes. Apartments, restaurants, cafes and clinics are a regular part of our work. We design the layout, custom joinery, lighting and materials, and prepare drawings and renders that a contractor can use.

09Which software do you use?

We model in Rhino. Every building is checked in a precise 3D model, the plans, sections and elevations are taken from it and finished as technical drawings, and the renders come from the same model. Alongside that we develop our own pattern-based design tools.
