A family apartment in Belgrade that keeps the character of a classic city interior, with herringbone parquet and ceiling rosettes, and pairs it with clean contemporary joinery. The day zone is one generous room: a living area with a piano and a full-height bookcase, a large dining table and a kitchen island in light stone.

In the night zone, glass partitions in slim black frames separate the bedroom, the dressing area and a small study without losing daylight. The bathrooms add warmth with terracotta joinery, stone-look tiles and black fittings.
