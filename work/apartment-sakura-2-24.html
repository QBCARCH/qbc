This Belgrade apartment takes a light, warm direction. Textured plaster walls, light oak and a long marble bench define the living room, where a fluted timber sliding panel hides the TV and turns the media wall into a bookcase when the screen is off.

The kitchen with its island sits in a softly lit niche, and the bedroom repeats the plaster and timber palette with a full-height headboard wall. The aim was a quiet, natural interior that is easy to live with every day.
