Studio

We design houses, apartments, clinics and hospitality spaces, and every project starts with patterns: the relationships of light, arrival, privacy and gathering that make a place work. The form comes after them, so it has a reason.

Since 2010

A Belgrade studio led by architects Slavoljub and Vladica Ljubić. We check every building in a precise 3D model in Rhino, take the drawings from it and render from the same model, so the images show what will be built. Alongside the projects we develop our own pattern-based design tools.
