An interior for a private clinic on Zlatibor, designed as a calm, hotel-like space rather than a clinical one. The reception and waiting lounge combine soft upholstered seating, walnut tables and plants with a large botanical wall finish behind glass, so the first impression is warmth instead of corridors and white tiles.

The treatment rooms continue the same palette of warm stone-coloured surfaces, timber and brass. Built-in counters with brass basins, concealed storage and soft pendant lighting keep the rooms practical for daily work, while the washrooms get a stronger character with dark marble and patterned wallpaper.
