Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains which personal details QBCARCHITECTS, an architecture studio in Belgrade, Serbia, collects through this website and in work with clients, why we use them and how you can ask us to correct or delete them. We are the controller of these data under the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection (Official Gazette of RS, No. 87/2018).

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our services. This includes the personal information you share through the contact form on this website or when you contact us by email, phone or messaging apps, such as your name, email address, phone number, project location and any project details or inquiries. Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to our email by the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect primarily to communicate with you and facilitate our services. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide initial architectural consultations.
  • Carry out the design work and architectural supervision you engage us for.
  • Send administrative information related to your project or our services.
  • Keep the website working correctly.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We respect the confidentiality of our clients. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We may share specific, necessary project information with trusted third-party consultants (such as structural, mechanical, or electrical engineers) and contractors exclusively for the purpose of executing your project. We may also disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Data Security

Inquiries are kept in our email account and project files, which only the studio can access. We keep them as long as they are needed for the inquiry or the project, and for as long as the law requires us to keep project records. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send documents you consider confidential through the form.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You can ask us at any time which of your personal data we hold, ask for them to be corrected or deleted, and withdraw your consent. If you think we have handled your data unlawfully, you can file a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Serbia.
