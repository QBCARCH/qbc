Services /

Architectural, Interior Design & Visualisation Services

We provide architectural, interior design and visualisation services, from the first concept to the finished building. One studio carries the project through design, BIM coordination, permits and supervision, so nothing gets lost between phases. Outside Serbia we work together with your local architect of record.

Process /

How we work

01

First conversation

We discuss your site, brief, budget and timeline, and check the planning documents that apply to the plot.

02

Proposal

A written scope of services, phases and fees, so you know what you get at each stage.

03

Concept

Massing, layouts and first visuals, refined with you until the direction is agreed.

04

Design and BIM

A coordinated Revit model with structure and MEP, material specification and cost control.

05

Permits and documents

Documentation for the permit process and for contractor bidding and construction.

06

On site

Site supervision, contractor questions and change control through to handover.

01

Conceptual & Schematic Design

We turn your brief into a clear spatial strategy. This phase covers site analysis, a review of planning conditions and the first architectural concepts, with a focus on layout, massing and the relationships between spaces.

Site Context Analysis

Evaluating topography, environmental conditions, and zoning regulations to inform the optimal design strategy.

Spatial Programming

Defining functional requirements and establishing highly efficient layouts for all project spaces.

Massing and Form Generation

Developing early 3D models to explore volumetric relationships and establish the architectural identity.

02

Design Development & BIM Modeling

We refine the design in a Building Information Model. Materials are selected, structure and building services are coordinated in the model, and clashes are resolved on screen before they can reach the construction site.

Advanced 3D Integration

Utilizing intelligent Building Information Modeling (BIM) to coordinate architecture, structure, and MEP systems.

Material Specification

Selecting finishes and systems that meet aesthetic goals while satisfying structural and budgetary constraints.

Proactive Clash Detection

Identifying and resolving spatial conflicts between engineering disciplines before construction begins.

03

Architectural Visualisation

Photorealistic images, from first massing studies to final material choices. Renders are built from the same BIM model as the drawings, so what you approve is what gets documented.

Concept Visuals

Early images that test massing, light and atmosphere before the design is fixed.

Material and Interior Studies

Close views of finishes, furniture and lighting, so decisions can be made with confidence.

Presentation Imagery

High-resolution images for investors, planning submissions and sales.

04

Construction Documents & Engineering

We produce detailed construction documentation: drawings, specifications and schedules that contractors can price and build from. Careful detailing means more accurate bids, fewer variations on site and documents that meet the applicable building codes.

Detailed Arch. Drawings

Producing precise floor plans, sections, and elevations required for accurate contractor bidding.

Custom Construction Details

Developing specialized technical drawings for complex structural junctions, thermal envelopes, and waterproofing.

Permit Documentation

Preparing documentation that meets the applicable building codes and supports the permit process, in Serbia through the CEOP e-permit system.

05

Construction Administration & Oversight

After the building permit is issued, clients can engage us to supervise the construction of our own projects. We visit the site, review contractor submittals and answer technical questions, so the building is built as it was designed.

Site Visits

Regular visits to check that the work follows the approved design and documentation.

Submittal Review

Reviewing shop drawings and material samples against the specification.

Design Queries and Changes

Answering contractors' technical questions and assessing proposed changes against the approved design.

06

Interior Architecture & Custom Detailing

We carry the architectural idea into the interior: custom joinery, lighting and the selection of materials, so that the building and its interior read as one.

Bespoke Millwork Design

Designing custom cabinetry and built-in elements tailored to the geometry of each space.

Lighting Design

Integrated lighting plans that support how each space is used.

Material Curation

Selecting interior finishes, fixtures and hardware that follow the architectural idea.

07

Feasibility Studies & Master Planning

For large-scale developments, we provide strategic master planning and feasibility analysis. We evaluate site potential, assess regulatory constraints, and develop long-term spatial strategies that maximize land value and guide future phases of development.

Site Potential Evaluation

Assessing physical constraints and regulatory frameworks to determine the maximum development capacity of a plot.

Yield and Density Analysis

Calculating buildable areas and optimizing spatial distribution to ensure the project is financially viable.

Long-Term Phasing Strategies

Structuring large-scale developments into logical stages to optimize capital investment and manage risk.

Questions /

Frequently asked questions

01Do you work with clients outside Serbia?

Yes. Concept design, interior design and architectural visualisation can be done fully remotely, with video meetings in English. For a building outside Serbia we work together with your local architect of record, who handles the permit in that country. For projects in Serbia we handle the whole process.

02What do you need to start a project?

The location of the plot or the property, any documents you already have for it, a short description of what you need, a rough budget and your target timeline. If you do not have all of it yet, send what you have and we will help you complete the brief.

03How are fees agreed?

You receive a written proposal before work begins. It lists the scope of services, the phases and the fee for each phase, so you know what you get and what it costs at every stage.

04How long does a project take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the project and on the permit procedure. A concept usually takes several weeks, and a full set of documents takes a few months. We give a schedule in the proposal and update it as the project moves forward.

05What is CEOP and how does it affect my project?

CEOP is the Serbian national e-permit system, through which building permits are applied for electronically. We know the regulations and the procedure well, so we plan the permit path from the first sketch and prepare documentation that meets the requirements.

06Do you supervise construction?

Yes, on our own projects. After the building permit is issued, clients can engage us to supervise the construction: site visits, review of contractor submittals and answers to technical questions. We are architects, not a contractor, so we do not build.

07Do you design interiors on their own?

Yes. Apartments, restaurants, cafes and clinics are a regular part of our work. We design the layout, custom joinery, lighting and materials, and prepare drawings and renders that a contractor can use.

08Which software and workflow do you use?

We work in Autodesk Revit. One BIM model drives the plans, sections, schedules and renders, and it is coordinated with structure and building services so that clashes are found on screen, before construction.
