A single-storey family house in a rural setting, made of two volumes in the shape of the traditional gabled house. The white rendered wing holds the private rooms, while the dark timber-clad wing holds the living area and opens to a large covered terrace under a deep gable frame lined with wood.

The two wings meet at a stone-clad entrance, which separates the day and night zones and frames a small courtyard. Simple forms and natural materials make the house feel at home in the landscape while giving it a clearly contemporary character.
