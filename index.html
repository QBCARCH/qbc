Since /2010

Operating from Belgrade, Serbia, QBC Architects is a professional design studio led by principal architect Slavoljub Ljubic. We specialize in residential and commercial architecture, prioritizing structural integrity, spatial functionality, and precise detailing. We provide investors with technical certainty through advanced BIM modeling in Autodesk Revit, strict compliance with local zoning regulations, and highly efficient project management.
