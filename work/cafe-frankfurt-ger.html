A narrow cafe in Frankfurt am Main, designed in May 2024 for LM Mitic GmbH. The space is a long room about nine metres deep and just over two metres wide, with a second, wider room behind it and a WC off the side.

The bar runs along the glazed end. Its front is pink brick, the wall behind it is stainless steel and fluted metal, and the stools are blue. Along the long wall there is an oak bench with steel tables in front of it, so the width of the room is shared between guests and the passage to the bar.

The renders show the interior. The layout sketch and the notes sketch are drawn over the CAD plan of the room (1:50); the furniture positions in them are a concept reading of the renders.
