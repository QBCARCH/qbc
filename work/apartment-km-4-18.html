Apartment KM 4/18 in Belgrade is the perfect blend of classic style with modern design touches. Clear lines and soft colors contribute to the elegance and comfort of the space, while the apartment is skillfully divided into two functional areas: the daytime and nighttime zones.

No items found.

The daytime zone is a truly multifunctional space that is used throughout the day. The kitchen with an island offers both practicality and style, ideal for cooking and socializing. The dining table, separate from the kitchen, allows for comfortable meals with family and friends. A particular charm to the living area is given by the authentic tiled stove, preserved from the past, which not only adds warmth to the space but also evokes the spirit of old Belgrade architecture.

The nighttime zone is designed as a spacious master bedroom providing peace and privacy. The room includes a functional workspace, ideal for those who work from home or enjoy relaxing with a book. By the window, there is a reading nook, perfect for enjoying natural light and the surrounding view.

With carefully balanced classic elements and modern comforts, Apartment KM 4/18 offers a harmonious living space that combines history and contemporary living. Ideal for those seeking a home with character and functionality in the heart of Belgrade. Can you imagine living here?
