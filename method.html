Method /

Patterns before form

How a QBCARCHITECTS project takes shape

Start a project→

A pattern is a relationship that repeats in good places: where light enters, how people arrive, where they gather and where they go to be alone, how a building meets the ground. Before we draw a form, we find the patterns of the site and of the brief. The form then answers them, one by one.

Slavoljub started working on this approach in 2009, in research on how settlements take shape. Since then it has guided the studio's projects, from apartments to complexes.

Today we are building it into our own design software. It lets us test far more variants of a layout at the start and compare them by clear criteria. How the tools work stays inside the studio. What the client gets is the result: an earlier, better argued choice, and fewer changes later.

Case study /

Trnava: from an orchard to a line

An event center with a restaurant and two guest houses, on a sloping orchard plot near Topola. The building permit is in progress. The diagrams follow the design from the site to the form.

01

The site

A long plot between the internal road and the orchard. The ground falls about three metres towards a lake at the lower end.

02

The patterns

Four relationships decided the plan. Guests arrive from the road at the upper end. The view and the quiet are at the lake. An event needs a room that opens to the outside. Guests who stay overnight need distance from the music.

03

The line

So the building became one line along the road: parking at the top, then the pool, services and kitchen, and the hall with its terrace at the lake end. The two guest houses stand apart, on the higher part of the plot.

04

The section

Where the ground drops, the hall rises: it stands a full storey above the terrain, with the basement beneath it. Along the line the roofs change height, from 7.05 to 3.50 metres, so the long building reads as a row of separate volumes.

05

The building

Two of those volumes carry the character of the project: a pitched hall in dark metal and a concrete box, joined by a low glazed link.

Four rules /

The method in practice

01

Patterns before form

Before the first sketch we study the patterns of the site and the brief: light, access, views, privacy, and how people will move and gather. They decide the layout, and the form follows from them.

02

One precise 3D model

We model the building in Rhino and check every dimension in 3D. Drawings and renders come from the same model, so they always agree.

03

Designed to be built

Structure, materials and construction method are tested from the first sketch, so the design survives contact with the site.

04

Cost awareness

We specify materials and layouts with the budget in view, and say early when a decision will cost more.
