We design houses, apartments, clinics and hospitality spaces, and every project starts with patterns: the relationships of light, arrival, privacy and gathering that make a place work. The form comes after them, so it has a reason.

How we work

The method in practice

  1. 01Patterns before formBefore the first sketch we study the patterns of the site and the brief: light, access, views, privacy, and how people will move and gather. They decide the layout, and the form follows from them.
  2. 02One precise 3D modelWe model the building in Rhino and check every dimension in 3D. Drawings and renders come from the same model, so they always agree.
  3. 03Designed to be builtStructure, materials and construction method are tested from the first sketch, so the design survives contact with the site.
  4. 04Cost awarenessWe specify materials and layouts with the budget in view, and say early when a decision will cost more.
